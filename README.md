## 🛠 What We Do
Cray Group LTD is a Kenyan software company dedicated to building powerful tools for ISPs and tech-driven businesses. We help clients automate their networks, manage users, and grow their revenue.

### 🧩 Core Products:
- ✅ **Hotspot Management System** – Built for MikroTik, with voucher generation, STK Push (M-Pesa), usage tracking, and more.
- ✅ **ISP Billing Platform** – Automated invoicing, due date tracking, and account suspension with FreeRADIUS integration.
- ✅ **Router Automation & Monitoring** – Scheduled backups, link monitoring, and alerting via SMS, Telegram, and email.
- ✅ **MikroTik Monitoring Tool** – Built with FastAPI, monitors interfaces and router status with SNMP and ping.
